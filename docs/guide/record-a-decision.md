# Record a decision

## What this page is for

Use this page to record a decision that differs from ATLAS's suggestion, or a decision to accept a piece of debt.

## Who uses it

Product manager, solution architect and audit.

## How to use it

1. Open the report item.
2. Write what you decided and why.
3. Save.

## Good to know

ATLAS keeps each decision. Decisions become test cases for later runs.
