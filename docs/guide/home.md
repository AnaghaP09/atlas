# Home

## What this page is for

Home is your starting point. It shows what needs your attention in the current project.

## Who uses it

Every role. Each role sees its own items.

## How to use it

1. Read the items at the top first. They need a decision or an action.
2. Check the mode, the kill switches and the money spent on AI calls.
3. Select an item to open its page.

## Good to know

Every item has a label that says where its data came from. See the [glossary](glossary.md).
