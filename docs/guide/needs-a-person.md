# Needs a person

## What this page is for

This page lists the items that ATLAS will not decide on its own.

## Who uses it

Engineering manager, security engineer and audit.

## How to use it

1. Read why the item is here. For example: low confidence, a blocking security finding, or text that looked like an instruction.
2. Open the evidence.
3. Make the decision on the page that the item points to.
