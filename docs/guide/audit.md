# Audit

## What you see

You see every page. You also see records that other roles cannot see, such as failed notification deliveries.

## What you decide

- Whether ATLAS may resume after a kill switch stopped it.
- Whether a published claim needs a correction.
- Which public repositories ATLAS watches as projects.

## What you do in ATLAS

1. Check the open incidents.
2. Record the proof that each incident needs.
3. Resume a kill switch only when ATLAS accepts the proof.
4. Switch between Connected and Offline mode.

## Requests that wait for you

Requests to read kill-switch incidents or audit pages wait for your decision. They appear in your notifications. Every request is kept in a record that you can read. See [Access requests](access-requests.md).
